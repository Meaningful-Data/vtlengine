:Tests: ``cast(Me_1, time_period, "\QQ-YYYY")`` on a String measure: the mask of the page's Example 9, ``cast ( "Q1-2000", time_period, "\QQ-YYYY" )``: the quarter before the year.
:Manual: `Type Conversion and Formatting Mask <https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/typical_behaviour.html#type-conversion-and-formatting-mask>`__: "The position of the digits in the mask denotes the position of the corresponding values", and "The special characters and the special words, if prefixed by the reverse slash [...] in the mask, appear in the same position in the time format".
:Expected: ``2000Q1``, "The first quarter of year 2000", and ``2001Q3``. Only the mask explains them: without it, the conversion starts with the four digits of the year, and these strings start with ``Q``.
