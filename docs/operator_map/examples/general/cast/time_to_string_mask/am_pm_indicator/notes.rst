:Tests: ``cast(Me_1, string, "DD.MM.YYYY hh:mm AM/PM/DD.MM.YYYY hh:mm AM/PM")`` on a Time measure whose start and end have times of day: the indicator of AM and PM, in capitals.
:Manual: `Type Conversion and Formatting Mask <https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/typical_behaviour.html#type-conversion-and-formatting-mask>`__: "AM/PM indicator of AM / PM (e.g. am/pm for “am” or “pm”)", "The case of the special word correspond to the case of the value", and "h hour digit (by default on 24 hours)".
:Expected: ``05.04.2018 09:30 AM/05.04.2018 12:30 PM`` for the time from ``2018-04-05T09:30:00`` to ``2018-04-05T12:30:00``. The manual does not say whether the indicator puts the hours on 12: ``09`` and ``12`` are the same hours on 12 and on 24 hours.
:Finding: Both engines raise SemanticError ``1-1-5-4``, "Invalid mask to cast from type Time to String.": vtlengine also splits the mask at the ``/`` of ``AM/PM``, and finds no two dates.
