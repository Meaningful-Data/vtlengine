:Tests: ``cast(Me_1, string, "PY\YM\MDD\DT{h}h\Hmm\M/YYYY-MM-DDThh:mm:ssZ")`` on a Time measure whose start and end have times of day: the manual's mask for "Duration and end".
:Manual: `Type Conversion and Formatting Mask <https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/typical_behaviour.html#type-conversion-and-formatting-mask>`__: "Duration and end, such as "P1Y2M10DT2H30M/2018-04-05T12:30:00+02"" with this mask, and "P designator of duration" among the "fixed characters used in the ISO 8601 standard which can appear as fixed characters in the relevant masks".
:Expected: ``P1Y2M10DT2H30M/2018-04-05T12:30:00Z`` for the time from ``2017-01-26T10:00:00`` to ``2018-04-05T12:30:00``: the ``Z`` of the mask, where the manual's value has ``+02``. The mask writes its first ``P`` without the reverse slash that would keep it from being the special character "P period indicator": the example reads it as the fixed "P designator of duration", as the manual's value does.
:Finding: Both engines refuse the mask with SemanticError ``1-3-13``, "Unsupported VTL construct: a time written with a duration in the mask [...]".
