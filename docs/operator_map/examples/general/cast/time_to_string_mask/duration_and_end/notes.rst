:Tests: ``cast(Me_1, string, "PY\YM\MDD\DT/YYYY-MM-DD")`` on a Time measure: the manual's mask for a time written with its "Duration and end", to the day.
:Manual: `Type Conversion and Formatting Mask <https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/typical_behaviour.html#type-conversion-and-formatting-mask>`__: "Duration and end, such as "P1Y2M10D/2018-04-05"" with this mask, "P designator of duration" among the "fixed characters used in the ISO 8601 standard which can appear as fixed characters in the relevant masks", and "Any other character can be used in the mask, meaning simply that it appears in the same position".
:Expected: ``P1Y2M10DT/2018-04-05`` for the time from ``2017-01-26`` to ``2018-04-05``: the ``T`` of the mask is written, though the manual's value does not have it. The mask writes its first ``P`` without the reverse slash that would keep it from being the special character "P period indicator": the example reads it as the fixed "P designator of duration", as the manual's value does.
:Finding: Both engines refuse the mask with SemanticError ``1-3-13``, "Unsupported VTL construct: a time written with a duration in the mask [...]".
