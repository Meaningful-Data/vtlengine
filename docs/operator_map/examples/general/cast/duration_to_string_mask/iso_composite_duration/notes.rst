:Tests: ``cast(Me_1, string, "\PYY\YM\MDD\DThh\Hmm\Mss\S")`` on a Duration measure: the mask of an "ISO 8601 composite duration".
:Manual: `Type Conversion and Formatting Mask <https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/typical_behaviour.html#type-conversion-and-formatting-mask>`__: "ISO 8601 composite duration: "P10Y2M12DT02H30M15S" (P stands for “period”)", with "VTL Mask: ``\PYY\YM\MDD\DThh\Hmm\Mss\S``", and "The numerical representation is assumed to be padded by leading 0 by default".
:Expected: A year, a month and a day, each in its part of the mask and the other parts zero: ``P01Y0M00DT00H00M00S``, ``P00Y1M00DT00H00M00S`` and ``P00Y0M01DT00H00M00S``. Only the mask explains them: without it, a duration is printed in ISO 8601, ``P1Y``, ``P1M`` and ``P1D``.
:Finding: Both engines refuse the mask with SemanticError ``1-3-13``, "Unsupported VTL construct: an ISO composite duration in the mask [...]": vtlengine reads a mask of a duration with ``P`` and ``p`` only, as a Duration of vtlengine is one period indicator.
