:Tests: ``cast(Me_1, date, "YYYY-\QQ-DD")`` on a String measure: the quarter, after the letter ``Q``, then the day within the quarter.
:Manual: `Type Conversion and Formatting Mask <https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/typical_behaviour.html#type-conversion-and-formatting-mask>`__: the special character "Q quarter", and "it is assumed that the more detailed units (e.g., the day) are expressed through the order number that they assume within the less detailed ones (e.g., the month and the year)".
:Expected: ``2018-04-05``, the 5th day of the second quarter of 2018, and ``2021-12-31``, the 92nd and last day of the fourth quarter of 2021.
:Finding: Both engines raise SemanticError ``1-1-5-4``, "Invalid mask to cast from type String to Date.": vtlengine takes no quarter in the mask of a date.
