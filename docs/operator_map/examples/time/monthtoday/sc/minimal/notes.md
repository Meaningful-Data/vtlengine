Manual: [Duration to number days](https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/operators/Time%20operators/Duration%20to%20number%20days/index.html), Example 3 (in the text of the page):
`monthtoday (P3M10D);` "returns 100": 3 × 30 + 10 ("according to ISO 8601 M = 30D"). The
duration is the cast of a string, as in `yeartoday/sc`.
