One operand is null, on the left or on the right. The page lists `false and null` as false,
`true and null` as null and `null and null` as null. The operator is symmetric, so `null and false`
is false and `null and true` is null.

- `true and null` is null
- `null and true` is null
- `false and null` is false
- `null and false` is false
