One operand is null, on the left or on the right. The page lists `false or null` as null,
`true or null` as true and `null or null` as null. The operator is symmetric, so `null or false` is
null and `null or true` is true.

- `true or null` is true
- `null or true` is true
- `false or null` is null
- `null or false` is null
