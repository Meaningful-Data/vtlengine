:Tests: ``calc At_1 := At_1 || "x"``: ``At_1``, a plain (not viral) Attribute of ``DS_1``, recalculated under its own name, with the role left out.
:Manual: `Inner Join <https://sdmx-twg.github.io/vtl/v2.2/reference_manual/operators/Join%20operators/Inner%20Join/index.html>`__: VDS₁ has the identifiers and the measures of the operands, and "The viral attributes, coming from any number of Data Sets, which appear once", and no other attribute, so the clause could not read ``At_1`` (error ``1-1-1-10``); in calc, "if the role is not specified, it will be measure".
:Expected: the join keeps ``(1,A)``, ``(1,B)`` and ``(2,A)`` (``(2,B)`` is in ``DS_1`` only), and ``At_1`` is a Measure: ``ax``, ``bx`` and ``cx``.
:Team decision: `#1258 <https://github.com/Meaningful-Data/vtlengine/issues/1258#issuecomment-6661169999>`__: the joins keep their non-viral Attributes until the VTL Task Force settles `sdmx-twg/vtl#730 <https://github.com/sdmx-twg/vtl/issues/730>`__, "Attributes in joins should be treated as measures": dropping them "may have many unintended implications in the use of non-viral attributes".
